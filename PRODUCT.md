## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and prospective employers evaluating Astha Prajapati for senior leadership roles in manufacturing operations, GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice), and project/sales management. They arrive to quickly judge career trajectory, credibility, and seniority, then decide whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio/career site for Astha Prajapati that presents her professional trajectory — from a Computer Engineering foundation and early software internships into a fast-moving leadership track in industrial operations (Sales Manager → Assistant General Manager at Eagle Pressure Systems in ~1 year) — to convince recruiters/employers of her readiness for further leadership roles and prompt them to make contact via LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A cross-disciplinary path that a purely technical or purely managerial candidate could not claim: a rigorous CS/software engineering foundation (Arduino, database administration, Django, ASP.NET MVC, neural networks/ML) that was deliberately redirected into hands-on industrial leadership, culminating in a rapid promotion to Assistant General Manager overseeing GMP and project management. The technical training is the origin story and differentiator; the leadership record is the headline claim.

## Operating Context

- Single confirmed source of truth for career facts: the LinkedIn profile pasted into the brief (https://www.linkedin.com/in/astha-prajapati-a71944177/).
- Visitors are time-pressed professionals scanning on desktop or mobile; they need employer names, titles, tenure, and location legible at a glance even inside a cinematic presentation.
- No CMS or backend — content is hardcoded from the confirmed record below.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with the existing scaffold: Next.js (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4. No additional framework decision needed.
- Delivered as a single, scroll-driven page ("single webpage" per brief) with cinematic, interactive visual storytelling — not a multi-route site.
- No real headshot is available yet. Do not fabricate or imply a real photo; use an abstract/typographic/illustrative treatment instead of a placeholder that looks like a missing real photo.
- No résumé/CV file, no testimonials, no performance metrics, and no confirmed email or phone number exist. Do not invent any of these. LinkedIn is the only real, confirmed contact channel and must be the primary contact CTA.
- Tagline/brand voice: Astha has no existing professional tagline. The user explicitly delegated creating one to this build — it must be written from the confirmed career facts, not invented biography.

5. LinkedIn (https://www.linkedin.com/in/astha-prajapati-a71944177/) is the single confirmed, real contact channel and must be a prominent, repeated CTA.
